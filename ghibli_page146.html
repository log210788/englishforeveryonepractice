Describing ability

Words such as "quite" and "very" are modifying adverbs. You can use them before other adverbs to give more information about how you do something.

New language Modifying adverbs
Vocabulary Skills and abilities
New skill Saying how well you do things
46.1

MARK THE SENTENCES THAT ARE CORRECT

Pedro is really good at history. / Pedro really good is at history.
You speak really well French. / You speak French really well.
Sandra is very good at singing. / Sandra very good is at singing.
Sal is at skiing quite good. / Sal is quite good at skiing.
Very well your uncle can swim. / Your uncle can swim very well.
They quite fast can run. / They can run quite fast.
Mr. Henderson is really good at golf. / At golf Mr. Henderson is really good.
