Talking about your town

Practice describing places in town and giving directions in the vibrant harbor town of Porto Soleil.

6.1

COMPLETE WITH 'A' OR 'THE'

Choose 'a' (first mention / general) or 'the' (specific / unique) for each sentence.

___ bank is on the main street. EXAMPLE
There is ___ post office near the school.
___ park is very beautiful in spring.
I need ___ map of the town.
___ train station is far from here.
6.2

GIVE DIRECTIONS — COMPLETE EACH SENTENCE

Fill in the blank using: turn left, turn right, go straight, go past, or take the second.

___ on Baker Street, then turn left. EXAMPLE
___ the church and the school is on your right.
___ at the traffic lights.
___ turning on the left.
___ at the roundabout.
