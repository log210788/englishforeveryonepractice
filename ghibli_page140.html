Unit 44

What you can and can't do

Use "can" to talk about the things you are able to do, such as ride a bicycle or play the guitar. Use "cannot" or "can't" for things you are not able to do.

New language "Can," "can't," and "cannot"
Vocabulary Talents and abilities
New skill Say what you can and can't do
44.1

FILL IN THE GAPS TO WRITE EACH SENTENCE THREE DIFFERENT WAYS

I can ride a horse. / I cannot ride a horse. / I can't ride a horse.
I can climb a tree. / I cannot climb a tree. / I can't climb a tree.
I can speak French. / I cannot speak French. / I can't speak French.
I can sing. / I cannot sing. / I can't sing.

I can swim. / I cannot swim. / I can't swim.
