Joining sentences

Use conjunctions like "and" to add information and "but" to show contrast between ideas.

CROSS OUT THE INCORRECT WORD IN EACH SENTENCE

1. There's a library, a store, / a café.
2. There's a castle and a church / there isn't a museum.
3. Pete eats apples / doesn't eat bananas.
4. Greg reads magazines / a newspaper.
5. I have a calendar / a notebook.
6. He goes swimming / he doesn't play soccer.
