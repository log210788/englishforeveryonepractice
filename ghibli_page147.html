Unit 7 • Lesson: Describing ability

Describing ability

Words such as "quite" and "very" are modifying adverbs. You can use them before other adverbs to give more information about how you do something.

New language Modifying adverbs
Vocabulary Skills and abilities
New skill Saying how well you do things
