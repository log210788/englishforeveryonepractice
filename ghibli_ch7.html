What you have & Accommodation

Practice talking about possessions and describing accommodation in the cozy Treehouse Village of Morinoki.

7.1

COMPLETE WITH 'HAVE' OR 'HAS'

Use 'have' for I/you/we/they and 'has' for he/she/it.

I ___ a big house. EXAMPLE
She ___ a beautiful garden.
We ___ three bedrooms.
He ___ a small apartment.
They ___ a swimming pool.
7.2

DESCRIBE THE ROOM — COMPLETE THE SENTENCE

Use 'There is' or 'There are' to describe what is in each room.

___ a sofa in the living room. EXAMPLE
___ two beds in the bedroom.
___ a big window in the kitchen.
___ four chairs around the table.
___ a bathtub in the bathroom.
