Units 2–3

Talking about yourself

Practice using "to be" correctly, talk about your nationality, age, and job in the beautiful coastal fishing village of Umikaze Bay.

2.1

COMPLETE EACH SENTENCE WITH THE CORRECT FORM OF "TO BE"

Choose am / is / are to complete each sentence. Listen to each example!

I ___ a student. EXAMPLE
She ___ from Japan.
We ___ happy students.
He ___ 25 years old.
They ___ teachers.
You ___ very kind.
2.2

REWRITE EACH SENTENCE AS A QUESTION

Turn each statement into a yes/no question by moving the verb to the front.

She is a doctor. EXAMPLE
He is from Brazil.
They are happy.
You are a teacher.
She is 30 years old.
We are students.
