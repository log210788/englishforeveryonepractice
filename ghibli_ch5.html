Describing your day & week

Practice talking about daily and weekly routines above the clouds in the magical floating city of Sora.

5.1

WRITE THE CORRECT VERB FORM

Add -s or -es to the verb for he/she/it. Write the full sentence.

She ___ (go) to school every day. EXAMPLE
He ___ (watch) TV in the evening.
She ___ (study) English on Mondays.
He ___ (have) lunch at noon.
She ___ (do) exercise every morning.
5.2

COMPLETE WITH A DAY OF THE WEEK

Write the correct day to complete each sentence about the weekly routine.

The day after Sunday is ___. EXAMPLE
The day before Saturday is ___.
The middle day of the work week is ___.
The last day of the weekend is ___.
The first day of the work week is ___.
